Where it stands today

The shipping icon reads as a cup on a stick. The bowl swallows the stem instead of crossing it, there are no prongs above the bowl, the foot is a wide slab, and a radial gradient inside a circle inside a maskable square wastes most of the tile. Each study below fixes at least one of those.

With psi

stem required 01–06

Construction rule held across all six: stem from the cap line to the foot, two prongs rising above the bowl to the same cap line, bowl joined at the stem's midpoint. The letter stays a letter.

Without psi

clinical objects 07–12

Psi is heavily used across psychology apps and reads as academic. These pull from what the toolkit actually does — scoring scales, severity thresholds, monitoring, dose math — and from the instruments in the room.
