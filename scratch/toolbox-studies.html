The kit, not the letter

Twelve marks built on the two ideas you picked: a case of instruments with the psi on it, and the psi that measures something. The reference draws the toolbox with three tools sticking out — that is a lot of detail for a 16px tile, so the set runs from the literal version down to marks that keep the toolbox read with two shapes. Every psi still carries its full stem.
