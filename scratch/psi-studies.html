The stem is the letter

The first set drew the descender at 6 units of 64 — the stem stopped almost as soon as it left the bowl, so the mark read as a cup on a stub. Every study here is rebuilt on one skeleton with a 16-unit descender and a stem spanning 81% of the canvas. The vertical is now the dominant stroke, which is what makes a Ψ a Ψ.
