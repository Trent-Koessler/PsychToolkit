PsychToolkit / angled toolbox / 12 marks

Twelve boxes on an axis

The reference case is drawn in three-quarter view — you see the lid, the front and a little of the top at once, and the tools lean out of it. Every mark here is built on one axonometric grid: a top face at 24.5°, verticals staying vertical, and any psi sheared onto the face it sits on so it belongs to the box rather than floating over it.

Render

One grid under all twelve

The top face is a diamond 44 wide and 20 deep; the two front faces drop 16 units from its lower edges. Anything drawn on the right face is sheared by −24.5° and anything on the left face by +24.5°, so the psi sits in the surface. Facet edges are cut out of the silhouette rather than drawn over it, which keeps every mark a single colour.

  • top face 44 × 20
  • drop 16
  • shear ±24.5°
  • crease cut 2.2
